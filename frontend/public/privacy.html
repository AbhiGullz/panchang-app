Gajaa Panchang

Privacy information

Gajaa Panchang provides location-based calendar calculations. This page explains what information the app uses and which services receive it. The app does not require an account.

Location and Panchang calculations

If you search for a place or choose to use your current location, the selected coordinates and time zone are used to calculate Panchang timings. Location search and reverse-geocoding requests are sent through the app service to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim geocoding service to find place names and coordinates. Browser location is requested only after you choose the current-location option.

Information stored on your device

Your selected language, location, calendar school, ayanamsa preference, selected date, and selected app tab are stored in browser storage so the app can remember your choices. Recent Panchang results are cached locally for offline use. These settings can be cleared by removing the site’s stored data in your browser.

Service providers and technical data

Requests must pass through the site’s hosting and network providers to deliver the app. Those providers may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details, and request time under their own privacy terms. OpenStreetMap’s privacy information is available at OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.

Advertising and consent

Gajaa Panchang is setting up Google AdSense. If advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, and personalize ads as permitted by your choices and applicable law. Visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland are shown a consent message through Google’s certified consent management platform. You can revisit or change consent choices through the privacy/consent controls made available on the site.

Children and contact

The app is a general calendar reference and is not designed to collect information directly from children. For a privacy question or data request, use the feedback option in the app and include “Privacy” in your message.

Updates

This notice may be updated when app features or service providers change. The current version is published on this page.
